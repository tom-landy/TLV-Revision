Topic overview podcasts (the short summary episodes) go in this folder.

Upload them with their original names, e.g.
  E1. Why_Organisational_Inertia_Guarantees_Failure.m4a
The site also accepts the name without the "E1. " part, or just E1.m4a (.mp3 works too).
Each episode appears in the Podcasts tab and on its topic page as soon as it is here.
The list of episodes (titles, topics, what they cover) is in public/data/podcasts.js.

Keep each file under 25 MB (GitHub's website limit). Play each to the end before uploading.
